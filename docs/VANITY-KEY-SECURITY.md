# ⚠️ What Someone Can Do With a Vanity Private Key

## Full Control = Complete Risk

If someone has your **vanity private key**, they have **COMPLETE CONTROL** over that wallet address. Here's what they can do:

### 1. **Steal All Funds** 💰
- Transfer ALL SOL from the wallet
- Transfer ALL tokens (SPL tokens, NFTs, etc.)
- Drain the wallet completely
- No way to stop them once they have the key

### 2. **Sign Any Transaction** ✍️
- Sign transactions as if they ARE you
- Create tokens in your name
- Interact with any Solana program
- Approve token transfers
- Delegate staking

### 3. **Impersonate Your Address** 🎭
- Use your vanity address for malicious purposes
- Create tokens that appear to be from you
- Scam people who trust your vanity address
- Damage your reputation

### 4. **Access Historical Data** 📊
- View all past transactions
- See all tokens you've held
- Track your trading patterns
- Analyze your wallet activity

### 5. **Control Associated Accounts** 🔗
- Access associated token accounts (ATAs)
- Transfer tokens from ATAs
- Close token accounts
- Manage all token-related operations

## Why Vanity Keys Are Valuable Targets

Vanity addresses (ending in "pump") are:
- **Memorable** - Easy to recognize and trust
- **Branded** - Associated with your project/brand
- **Public** - Often shared publicly for marketing
- **High-value** - May hold significant funds

This makes them **prime targets** for attackers.

## Protection Measures

1. **Never Share Private Keys** - Not even partially
2. **Use Hardware Wallets** - For high-value vanity addresses
3. **Rotate Keys** - If you suspect compromise
4. **Monitor Balances** - Set up alerts
5. **Use Multi-Sig** - For critical operations
6. **Keep Keys Offline** - Store in secure, offline locations

## If Your Vanity Key is Compromised

1. **Immediately transfer all funds** to a new wallet
2. **Create a new vanity address** (if needed)
3. **Announce the compromise** to prevent impersonation
4. **Monitor the old address** for suspicious activity
5. **Consider it permanently compromised** - never use it again

## Bottom Line

**A private key = Complete ownership of that wallet address**

There is NO way to revoke or recover from a compromised private key. Once someone has it, they have full control forever.
